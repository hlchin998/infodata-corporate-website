Compliance

Compliance Confidence for Cross-Border Onboarding

Verifying Chinese nationals and businesses is a compliance challenge as much as a technical one. Info Data provides identity verification and source-based company records for Chinese individuals and businesses, helping financial institutions review information during onboarding and due diligence.

The Compliance Challenge

Why Verifying Chinese Customers Is Harder

Financial institutions in Malaysia must know who they are dealing with before they open an account, extend credit or enter a business relationship. When the customer is a Chinese national or a Chinese company, that is harder to do. Info Data is built to address each of these gaps.

Identity documents and corporate records are issued in China and don't fit standard local verification checks.

Standard verification tools may not cover all China issued identity documents, corporate registration records or relevant publicly available legal records.

For corporate clients, ownership can be layered, making it hard to see who ultimately controls the business.

Personal and corporate data must cross borders securely and in line with data protection requirements.

Our Support

How Info Data Supports Your Compliance Program

Know Your Customer: verified individuals

Our KYC solution verifies identity against authoritative national and telecom data sources. It combines name, ID card, facial liveness, mobile number and bank card checks, so you can confirm that the person is real and is who they claim to be.

Know Your Business: verified companies

Our KYB report consolidates available company registration, shareholders, litigation, financial, tax and operational records, helping users review the company's documented information and ownership structure.

Ultimate Beneficial Owner transparency

We trace layered ownership structures to the individuals who ultimately control or benefit from a company, presented in a clear ownership mapping chart. This helps your team identify beneficial owners and highlight ownership relationships for further review.

Additional Verification Checks

Additonal checks may include available legal and corporate records, identity-to-mobile-number matching and other source-based verification results. Findings are presented as factual records or verification outcomes for review by the customer.

Documented, defensible decisions

Results come from authoritative sources and are delivered in a single consolidated report. Each decision is backed by evidence you can show an auditor.
