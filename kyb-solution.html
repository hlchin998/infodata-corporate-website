Everything You Need to Know About a Chinese Business

Ten report categories covering ownership, finances, legal standing, and operations — in one consolidated view.

Company Registration

Builds a complete corporate profile — legal existence, registration number, and status verified against official Chinese business registries — alongside capital contribution details and social security information.

Shareholders

Maps the full shareholder structure to reveal who holds equity and in what proportion, identifying key personnel and identify recorded ownership links and potential beneficial ownership for further review.

Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO)

Maps available ownership records to help identify individuals who may ultimately own or control the company, visualised through a clear UBO mapping chart.

Financial Information

Surfaces balance sheet and income statement data, tracks year-on-year changes, and presents available company financial statements and reported financial indicators for customer review.

Tax Information

Reviews corporate tax records, tax credit rating, tax arrears announcements, tax violations, and abnormal account status — displays available corporate tax records, published tax-related notices and recorded tax compliance information.

Operation Information

Reviews business scope, staff size, and operating status, while listing available records of abnormal operations, serious violations, equity pledges, movable property mortgages, and judicial auctions.

Litigation Records

List available litigation and enforcement records, covering court opening notices, legal documents involved, and equity freeze records.

Judicial News

Monitors publicly reported judicial and legal information, including court announcements, dishonest information, and judicial involvement records.

Intellectual Property

Lists registered patents, trademarks, software copyright, and copyright of works, alongside website record filing and telecommunication license status.

Foreign Trading Information

Reveals cross-border trade relationships through outward investment, overseas investment, and guarantees extended to external parties.
