About Info Data

Bridging Malaysia and China for Safer Financial Onboarding

A Malaysia-based identity and business due diligence provider, built to verify Chinese national individuals and companies with confidence.

Our Story

Why We Started Info Data

Malaysian financial institutions need a reliable way to verify Chinese national customers against China-issued identity documents. Too often, that gap is filled with checks that stop at the basics.

Info Data supports cross-border identity verification and business due diligence through a platform designed to help organisations verify Chinese individuals and companies. Our services support customer onboarding, identity consistency checks and access to relevant company information, subject to applicable laws and data-source permissions.

What We Do

Two Solutions, One Cross-Border Platform

Built specifically to verify Chinese national individuals and Chinese companies from Malaysia.

China KYC: verify individuals

For Chinese national customers undergoing digital onboarding in Malaysia, our China KYC service supports identity document verification, facial verification where available, mobile number verification and bank card verification. Additional checks can support identity consistency, fraud prevention and compliance screening, subject to data availability and authorised use.

China KYB: verify businesses

For due diligence on Chinese companies seeking financial services, trade financing, or partnerships in Malaysia. Our consolidated China KYB report brings together available company registration details, ownership information, litigation records and other relevant corporate records to support business verification and due diligence.
